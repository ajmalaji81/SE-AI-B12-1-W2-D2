## 2. Environment & Worldbuilding

* **Terrain:** Churned, cracked red laterite soil (*Semman*), parched dry shrubs, trampled thorny scrub.
* **Atmosphere:** Thick haze of kicked-up red earth, burning black oil/diesel smoke, floating embers, and white cannon powder.
* **Backdrop Architecture:**
  * Looming 10th-century stone temple *Gopurams* and granite monolith pillars carved with Yali motifs, partially chipped by artillery.
  * Far background: Scorched horizon beneath a blazing, dust-choked midday sky.

## 3. Faction Profiles & Assets

### A. The Imperial Chola Host (*Chola Perunsenai*)

#### 1. Supreme Commander: Emperor Raja Raja Cholan I (Arulmozhivarman)
* **Mount:** Massive Asian tusker elephant (*Pattathu Yaanai*) adorned with bronze forehead plating (*Nettippattam*), red silk caparisons, tusk-mounted steel sabers, and a tiered gilded wooden *Howdah*.
* **Expression & Stance:** Unflinching, stone-cold sovereign authority; seated upright in high command without fear.
* **Attire & Armor:**
  * **Chestplate:** Cast-bronze cuirass sculpted with an embossed, roaring Tiger (*Puli kodi*) emblem and interlaced Dravidian knotwork.
  * **Garments:** Heavy crimson (*Arakku*) silk *dhoti/veeshti* trimmed with double-woven pure gold Zari borders.
  * **Ornaments:** Dual-strand *Rudraksha* malas encased in solid gold caps, jeweled armlets (*Tholu Valai*), heavy gold ear studs (*Kadukkan*), and a high-domed warrior crown-turban (*Mudi-Thalapa*) set with uncut rubies and emeralds.
  * **Weaponry:** High-carbon Wootz steel curved talwar-style Chola sword (*Urunda Vaal / Vettuvaal*), circular buckler shield of beaten bronze with brass rivets.

#### 2. Chola Regular Troops & Vanguard
* **Velaikkarar (Elite Guard):** Bare-chested veteran berserkers wearing thick leather chest harnesses, bronze forearm vambraces, saffron/crimson waist wraps, wielding two-handed greatswords and leaf-bladed spears (*Vel*).
* **Elephant Corps (*Yaanaippadai*):** Armored war elephants forming a protective wall, trunks reinforced with chain mail.
* **Archers & Infantry:** Foot soldiers equipped with long bamboo recurve bows (*Vil*) with fire-tipped forged bodkin arrows, wicker and leather shields painted with the jumping tiger banner.
* **Regimental Flags:** Massive fluttering triangular crimson silk standards bearing the imperial seated tiger emblem flanked by twin fish and bow symbols.

### B. British Imperial Expeditionary Force (Late 1930s Colonial Corps)

#### 1. Field Commander
* **Identity:** British Field Brigadier in khaki tropical drill uniform, Sam Browne leather belt, brass insignia, trench binoculars, and a Wolseley pith helmet.
* **Command Post:** Positioned behind a sandbag trench redoubt alongside a Rolls-Royce armored car or Morris-Commercial field artillery tractor.

#### 2. Mechanized & Regular Units
* **Infantry:** British and colonial sepoy regiments in khaki drill tunics, shorts, puttees, Brodie helmets, armed with Short Magazine Lee-Enfield (SMLE) No. 1 Mk III rifles and fixed bayonets.
* **Heavy Firepower:**
  * Vickers .303 water-cooled heavy machine guns mounted on tripods behind sandbags, expending linked brass casings into the red dirt.
  * Ordnance QF 18-pounder or 25-pounder field guns firing high-explosive shells, muzzle flashes kicking up dust rings.
* **Armor:** Early Cruiser or Vickers Light Tank Mk VI rumbling through the scrub, exhaust plumes mixing with black artillery smoke.

## 5. Audio & Sound Design Blueprint

* **Chola Soundscape:**
  * Deep resonant brass fanfares of *Kombu* (curved war horns) and thunderous *Thudumbu*, *Parai*, and *Murasu* battle drums.
  * Metallic clatter of bronze shields and unsheathing of Wootz steel blades.
  * Trumpeting elephants and deep war chants in classical Tamil: *"Vetrivél, Veeravél!"*

* **British Soundscape:**
  * Rhythmic mechanical chattering of Vickers .303 machine guns.
  * Heavy percussive boom of 25-pounder artillery batteries and whine of incoming shells.
  * Clatter of tank treads against rock; distant brass bugles.

* **Music Score:**
  * Hybrid orchestral fusion: Traditional Tamil folk percussion and *Nadaswaram* motifs colliding with low-end brass, dissonant cellos, and industrial war synths.
